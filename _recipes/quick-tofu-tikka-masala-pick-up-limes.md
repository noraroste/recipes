## Ingredienser

- 1 Tbsp vegetable oil
- 8.818 oz firm tofu
- 1 Tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 medium onion
- 1 Tbsp grated fresh ginger
- 1 tsp brown mustard seeds
- 2 Tbsp tikka masala spice mix
- ½ tsp salt
- ¼ tsp chili powder
- 1 large tomato
- ¾ cup soy cooking cream
- ½ cup unsweetened soy yogurt
- ¼ cup raw cashews
- 3 Tbsp tomato paste
- ½ lime
- 2 vegan naan

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Heat the oil in a large pan over high heat. Cook the tofu, turning periodically, until golden on all sides.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a sauté pan over medium-high heat. Cook the onion for 3 minutes.
3. Add the ginger and mustard seeds. Cook for 2 more minutes.
4. Add the masala seasoning, salt, and chili and toast for another minute. The dish is spicy, so omit or add less chili if you're not a fan of heat.
5. Add the chopped tomato, cooking cream, yogurt, cashews, and tomato paste. Cook for 7 to 8 minutes.
6. Lastly, add the cooked tofu and stir it through.
7. Squeeze over the lime juice, stir, and serve with naan or rice. Enjoy!
