## Ingredienser

- 16 oz Korean wheat noodles (or frozen/dried noodles (see notes)*)
- 1  yellow onion (diced)
- 1 cup diced zucchini
- 3 cups shredded cabbage
- 6-8  shiitake mushrooms (sliced)
- 4-5 tablespoons chunjang (Korean black bean paste)
- 1 tablespoon vegetarian oyster sauce
- 1 tablespoon sugar (or any other sugar/sweetener of choice )
- 1 cup vegetable broth
- 1 14 oz block extra firm tofu  (drained and diced into small cubes)
- 1 tablespoon cornstarch
- 2 tablespoons water

## Fremgangsmåte

1. Cook noodles according to the package&#39;s instructions. Drain and set aside.
2. Heat up ½ tablespoon of oil in a large cast iron skillet or non-stick pan over medium-high heat.
3. Once oil is heated, add onion and zucchini. Fry until onion is translucent, about 2-3 minutes.
4. Add cabbage and shiitake mushrooms next. Cook until veggies have softened, about 2-3 minutes.
5. Make a well in the pan, then add chunjang, oyster sauce, and sugar. Fry for a minute, stirring constantly.
6. Now, add broth and tofu to the pan. Give everything a mix, and bring mixture to a gentle simmer, cooking for 3-4 minutes.
7. In the meantime, mix cornstarch with water and stir until the cornstarch has dissolved completely.
8. Pour cornstarch slurry into the pan, and cook until the sauce has thickened.  You might need to add more broth or water to thin out the sauce.
9. Give it a taste, adding some soy sauce or salt if needed. Serve it with cooked noodles, and your vegan jajangmyeon is ready to be enjoyed!
