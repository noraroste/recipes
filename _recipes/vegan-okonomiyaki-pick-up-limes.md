Remove them from the pan. Wipe the pan clean, it'll be used to make the pancakes.", 'Add the tofu to a large bowl. Mash using a fork, or optionally, blend using an immersion blender until puréed.', 'Then add the flour, baking powder, and cooled kombu-broth to the large bowl*. Stir to combine.', 'Add the cabbage, green onions, and grilled mushrooms. Stir until everything is coated in batter.', 'Heat some oil in the large pan over medium-high heat. Cover the pan with a lid and cook until the bottom is golden, about 4 minutes.', 'Gently flip the okonomiyaki, cover again, and cook on the other side until golden.', 'Transfer to a plate, drizzle with okonomiyaki sauce, mayo, and any other desired toppings.
